# References and Orientation Leads

Find background reading by topic. See the [framework overview](./02-Framework-Overview.md) for the connection to the code.

## Arrow of time / Boltzmann entropy / Past Hypothesis
- Lebowitz, J. L. "Boltzmann's entropy and time's arrow." *Physics Today* 46(9):32–38 (1993).
- Goldstein, S. [Boltzmann's Approach to Statistical Mechanics](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0105242). Preprint (2001).
- Goldstein, S., Tumulka, R., & Zanghì, N. [Is the Hypothesis About a Low Entropy Initial State of the Universe Necessary for Explaining the Arrow of Time?](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.05601) *Physical Review D* 94, 023520 (2016).

## Information Theory & Hypothesis Testing
- Cover, T. M. & Thomas, J. A. *Elements of Information Theory*. Wiley-Interscience, 2nd ed. (2006), Chapter 11.
- Chernoff, H. "A measure of asymptotic efficiency for tests of a hypothesis based on the sum of observations." *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 23(4):493–507 (1952). MONAD's testing exponent cites this work; the [mathematical core](./03-Math-Core.md#7-information-theoretic-bounds) explains when that result applies.

## Inverse problems / well-posedness
- Hadamard, J. [Sur les problèmes aux dérivées partielles et leur signification physique](https://illposed.net/hadamard.pdf). *Princeton University Bulletin* 13(4), 49–52 (1902). On existence, uniqueness and stability.

## Open quantum systems / irreversibility as reduction
- Lindblad, G. "On the generators of quantum dynamical semigroups." Commun. Math. Phys. (1976).

## Records / decoherence / objectivity
- Zurek, W. H. "Decoherence, einselection, and the quantum origins of the classical." Rev. Mod. Phys. (2003).
- Zurek, W. H. [Quantum Darwinism](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.5082). *Nature Physics* 5, 181–188 (2009).

## Reversibility and fidelity decay
- Gorin, T., Prosen, T., Seligman, T. H., Žnidarič, M. "Dynamics of Loschmidt echoes and fidelity decay." Physics Reports (2006).

## Learning as stochastic/open dynamics
- Mandt, S., Hoffman, M. D., Blei, D. M. "Stochastic Gradient Descent as Approximate Bayesian Inference." (2017).

## Representation geometry / graph Laplacians
- Belkin, M. & Niyogi, P. [Laplacian Eigenmaps for Dimensionality Reduction and Data Representation](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/6789755). *Neural Computation* 15(6), 1373–1396 (2003).
- von Luxburg, U. "A tutorial on spectral clustering." Statistics and Computing (2007).

## Delay embedding / observability intuition
- Sauer, T., Yorke, J. A., Casdagli, M. "Embedology." J. Stat. Phys. (1991).

## Information loss and irreversibility in computation
- Landauer, R. "Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process." IBM J. R&D (1961).
- Bennett, C. H. "Logical Reversibility of Computation." IBM J. R&D (1973).

## Lorentz violation constraints (optional branch)
- Mattingly, D. "Modern Tests of Lorentz Invariance." Living Reviews in Relativity (2005).
- Kostelecký, V. A., Russell, N. "Data Tables for Lorentz and CPT Violation." (arXiv:0801.0287; updated regularly).
